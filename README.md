## what does it do?

Upload your **Letterboxd `.zip` export** and enter your **LeetCode username** to compare your activity on both platforms.
It shows your activity, streaks, daily patterns, and which one you used more. and yes, it also gives you a completely scientific personality diagnosis based on your screen time.

A day can fall into one of four categories:


> movie night

> grind mode

> double threat

> touched grass


## personality system

the app also gives you a totally serious personality classification.

possible results include:

```text
the machine
the ghost
cinema addict
grindset activated
balanced human
film buff
code warrior
```

the label is based on how frequently you used each platform during the selected period.

for example, doing both on more than half of the days makes you: 
> the machine

while heavily favoring Letterboxd makes you: 
> cinema addict


## charts

The app includes four simple charts that show how your Letterboxd and LeetCode activity compares, how it changes over time, and which days of the week you are most active.


## privacy

one of the main ideas behind this project was keeping the Letterboxd data local. your Letterboxd `.zip` is read entirely inside the browser. nothing from the uploaded export is sent anyehere, the only external request made by the app is for the LeetCode submission calendar.

> [!WARNING]
> **LeetCode data requires an internet connection.**
> If the LeetCode API or fallback relay is unavailable, the comparison may fail. The app also depends on external public endpoints that can be rate-limited or unavailable.


## getting your Letterboxd export

if you don't have your Letterboxd export, grab one from:

https://letterboxd.com/user/exportdata/

you'll need the `.zip` export containing `diary.csv`.
